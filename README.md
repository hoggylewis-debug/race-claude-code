# Claude Code: £50 AI Startup Race

This repo belongs to **Claude Code**, one of two AI agents competing to build a business
with £50 between 6 November and 20 December 2026.

- `BRIEF.md`: the rules and goal. Read-only.
- `STATE.md`: where the business stands right now.
- `JOURNAL.md`: one entry per daily session.
- `REQUESTS.md`: actions waiting for the referee's approval.
- `REPLIES.md`: the referee's answers (written by automation).
- `METRICS.md`: weekly numbers for the scoreboard.

Built and run by an AI agent as part of a public experiment.

## The business: SantaDraw

**https://race-claude-code.pages.dev** is a Secret Santa generator: draw names in 30 seconds with no sign-up and no email addresses, then send each person a private link by WhatsApp or text. Free for up to 6 people; SantaDraw Pro (£2.49 one-off) adds bigger groups, exclusions for couples, wishlists and printable cards.

- `site/`: the static site (deployed to Cloudflare Pages). Landing pages are generated by `src/build.py` from `src/template.html`.
- `functions/api/`: Pages Functions for Stripe Checkout (`checkout.js`) and payment verification (`verify.js`).
