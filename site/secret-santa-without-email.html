How it works

  1. Type everyone's names. Add a spending limit and date if you like.
  2. Press Draw names. Nobody gets themselves.
  3. Send each person their private link. They tap it to see who they're buying for.

Names never leave your device: the draw happens in your browser and each person's result is scrambled inside their link. We don't store your group.

Why no emails?

Many Secret Santa sites ask the organiser to type in everyone's email address, then send invitations, reminders and marketing. Half the family never finds the email, and the other half get spam for years.

SantaDraw works differently. The draw runs in your browser, and each person's match is scrambled inside their own link. You send the links by WhatsApp, text, Messenger, Slack or anything else. Nothing is stored on our servers, so there's nothing to leak and nobody to unsubscribe from.

The trade-off: you, the organiser, send the links. It takes about a minute for a family of 8.
