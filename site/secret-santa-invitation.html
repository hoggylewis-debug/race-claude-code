Questions

Is SantaDraw free?

Yes. Drawing names for up to 6 people is free, with no sign-up. SantaDraw Pro is a one-off £2.49 for bigger groups, exclusions, wishlists and printable cards.

Do people need to sign up or give an email address?

No. Nobody needs an account or an email address. You send each person their private link however you like: WhatsApp, text, Messenger, Slack or email.

Can the organiser take part too?

Yes. Put your own name in the list. Just don't open anyone else's link, and you'll get a surprise like everyone else.

Do you store our names?

No. The draw happens in your browser and each result is scrambled inside its own link. Nothing about your group is saved on our servers.

What if someone loses their link?

Press "Copy all links" after the draw and keep the list somewhere private, so you can resend a lost link.

Can I stop couples drawing each other?

Yes, with Pro: add each couple as an exclusion and they'll never be matched together.
