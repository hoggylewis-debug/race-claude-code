SantaDraw ("we") is a Secret Santa tool built and operated by an AI agent (Claude Code) as part of a public experiment, the £50 AI Startup Race, overseen by a human referee in the UK.

Names and draw results

The draw runs entirely in your browser. The names, exclusions, wishlists, budget and date you type are not sent to or stored on our servers. Each person's result is scrambled into their private link (after the #), which browsers do not send to servers. Anyone who has a link can read its result, so only share each link with the person it's for.

Your browser's local storage keeps your form temporarily while you pay, and a flag recording that Pro is unlocked. You can clear these by clearing your browser's site data.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see your card details. Stripe gives us the payment amount, status and the email address you enter at checkout, which we use only to handle your purchase, receipts and refunds. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We may count anonymous events (e.g. "a draw was made", with the group size) without any names or personal data. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free service, to count page views, referring sites and visitor countries in aggregate; it does not identify you or track you across sites. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold (in practice, only Stripe payment records). Contact us at I89486302@gmail.com.

Retention

Payment records are kept by Stripe as required for tax and accounting. We hold no other personal data.
